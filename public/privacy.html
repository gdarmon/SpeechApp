Your privacy

Updated 21 September 2026. Fala is operated by the developer reachable at gdarmon@gmail.com.

Your account and learning data

Google sign-in gives Fala a Google account identifier and your email address. Fala uses the identifier to keep your account separate from other learners. We do not receive your Google password, contacts, or email messages.

Fala saves conversation transcripts, AI replies, learning feedback, approximate speaking time, assessment results, and practice patterns to provide your conversations and progress across devices. Your account is private to you; the service operator and hosting providers process this data to run the service.

Speech and AI

The native Android app uses your phone's speech recognition and Brazilian Portuguese voice playback. The native Android app does not create or upload audio recordings to Fala. If you enable network recognition, your phone's speech provider may receive audio. Voice playback may also use the network, depending on your installed voice.

Online web practice: with your microphone permission, the website records a short answer while you hold the button. When you release, the recording is sent over HTTPS through Fala to Groq for transcription in the current deployment (or OpenAI when explicitly configured). Fala does not save the audio file in its database or application logs. You can edit the transcription before sending your conversational answer. Portuguese replies are sent to OpenAI for AI-generated voice playback. Groq and OpenAI process requests under their respective data and retention policies; setting store:false on conversation requests does not establish Zero Data Retention.

Conversation text and relevant learning context are sent to Groq in the current deployment (or the configured alternative AI provider) to generate replies and feedback. The service is hosted on Netlify and uses Supabase to host its PostgreSQL database. Hosting, database, Google sign-in, and AI providers may process data outside your country under their own retention policies. Contact us for the providers and retention settings in the current deployment before sharing sensitive information.

Storage and security

Requests use encrypted HTTPS. The Android app encrypts its session credential using Android Keystore and excludes app data from Android backups. The website uses a Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookie for sign-in. Browser storage holds language and answer-visibility preferences; its offline cache contains only public app files, never recordings or private conversations. The server stores a hash of the session credential; sessions expire after 90 days. Short-lived hashed network-address counters protect sign-in from abuse. Fala's application code does not log conversation text or use advertising or analytics SDKs. Hosting providers may keep operational network logs.

Conversations remain in the active database until you delete them. Account deletion removes your account, device sessions, conversations, and learning data from the active database. Deletion from the app does not instantly erase existing database backups or logs retained by external providers; their configured retention periods apply. Contact us for a deletion or retention question.

Younger learners

Online AI practice is for ages 13 and up, with parent or guardian permission under 18. The age checkbox is a self-declaration, not verified age assurance. Fala has not enabled an approved under-13 AI service; do not use online AI mode to submit a younger child’s recordings or personal information.

Practice rewards, friends and reminders

Fala stores your practice points, qualifying practice dates, theme choices, optional nickname, time zone and reminder preferences. Joining a private circle shares your nickname and weekly practice totals with its members. Conversation text, recordings and email addresses are not shared with friends. Anyone with the invitation can join until the circle is full or its creator replaces the invitation. Leaving a circle removes your membership; closing a circle removes it for everyone.

Reminders are optional. Connecting browser notifications stores a delivery endpoint and encryption keys, processed by your browser's push provider to deliver notifications. Disconnecting removes that browser subscription. Turning off reminders stops future delivery attempts. Android asks for notification permission when required. Reminders skip days when you have already completed your daily goal.

Deleting an individual conversation keeps its aggregate practice rewards, without retaining its transcript in the rewards ledger. Clearing all learning data resets points, streaks and earned looks. Deleting your account also deletes rewards, preferences, subscriptions and circle memberships; circles you created are closed.

Reporting AI content

You can report an AI reply or review without leaving Fala. A report stores the selected AI content, the category you choose, your optional note, and your account and conversation identifiers so the operator can review it. Please do not include sensitive personal information. Reports are not shared with other learners. Deleting the related conversation or your account removes its reports from the active database.

Your choices

You can request deletion of your learning data while keeping your Fala account. This page explains the steps, what is removed and what is retained.

In the browser, you can revoke microphone permission, sign out, or use the linked account deletion page. In the Android app, you can disable network recognition, revoke microphone access in Android settings, delete a conversation, clear your learning data, sign out, or delete your Fala account in Settings. You can also delete your account on the web, without reinstalling the app. Deleting Fala does not delete your Google account.

Questions or a deletion request: gdarmon@gmail.com. Please do not email passwords, sign-in codes, or conversation transcripts.
