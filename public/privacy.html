Your privacy

Updated 20 September 2026. Fala is operated by the developer reachable at gdarmon@gmail.com.

Your account and learning data

Google sign-in gives Fala a Google account identifier and your email address. Fala uses the identifier to keep your account separate from other learners. We do not receive your Google password, contacts, or email messages.

Fala saves conversation transcripts, AI replies, learning feedback, approximate speaking time, assessment results, and practice patterns to provide your conversations and progress across devices. Your account is private to you; the service operator and hosting providers process this data to run the service.

Speech and AI

The native Android app uses your phone's speech recognition and Brazilian Portuguese voice playback. The native Android app does not create or upload audio recordings to Fala. If you enable network recognition, your phone's speech provider may receive audio. Voice playback may also use the network, depending on your installed voice.

Online web practice: with your microphone permission, the website records a short answer while you hold the button. When you release, the recording is sent over HTTPS through Fala to OpenAI for transcription. Fala does not save the audio file in its database or application logs. You can edit the transcription before sending your conversational answer. Portuguese replies are sent to OpenAI for AI-generated voice playback. OpenAI may retain data under its applicable abuse-monitoring policies; setting store:false on conversation requests does not establish Zero Data Retention.

Conversation text and relevant learning context are sent to the configured AI provider to generate replies and feedback. The service is hosted on Netlify and uses a PostgreSQL database. Hosting, database, Google sign-in, and AI providers may process data outside your country under their own retention policies. Contact us for the providers and retention settings in the current deployment before sharing sensitive information.

Storage and security

Requests use encrypted HTTPS. The Android app encrypts its session credential using Android Keystore and excludes app data from Android backups. The website uses a Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookie for sign-in. Browser storage holds language and answer-visibility preferences; its offline cache contains only public app files, never recordings or private conversations. The server stores a hash of the session credential; sessions expire after 90 days. Short-lived hashed network-address counters protect sign-in from abuse. Fala's application code does not log conversation text or use advertising or analytics SDKs. Hosting providers may keep operational network logs.

Conversations remain in the active database until you delete them. Account deletion removes your account, device sessions, conversations, and learning data from the active database. Deletion from the app does not instantly erase existing database backups or logs retained by external providers; their configured retention periods apply. Contact us for a deletion or retention question.

Younger learners

Online AI practice is for ages 13 and up, with parent or guardian permission under 18. The age checkbox is a self-declaration, not verified age assurance. Fala has not enabled an approved under-13 AI service; do not use online AI mode to submit a younger child’s recordings or personal information.

Your choices

In the browser, you can revoke microphone permission, sign out, or use the linked account deletion page. In the Android app, you can disable network recognition, revoke microphone access in Android settings, delete a conversation, clear your learning data, sign out, or delete your Fala account in Settings. You can also delete your account on the web, without reinstalling the app. Deleting Fala does not delete your Google account.

Questions or a deletion request: gdarmon@gmail.com. Please do not email passwords, sign-in codes, or conversation transcripts.
