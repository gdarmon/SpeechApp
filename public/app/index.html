How points work

1 XP per Portuguese reply, plus 1 XP when you speak it. Up to 20 XP per ten-answer lesson and 40 XP per day. Mistakes and answer ideas still count. Daily goals and weekly milestones celebrate practice without extra points.

Practice points unlock permanent looks. Your speaking level follows your independent answers. One missed day per week can protect a streak, and is marked ◇. Protected days earn no points.
