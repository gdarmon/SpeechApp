Delete your learning data

You can delete your Fala conversations and learning history while keeping your Fala account. Choose either method below.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email gdarmon@gmail.com from the Google email address you use to sign in to Fala.
  2. Ask us to delete all your learning data and keep your account. To remove only a particular conversation, identify it by its date or topic.
  3. We will verify account ownership and confirm when your request has been processed. Never send your password, sign-in codes or conversation transcripts.
Request data deletion

No app installation is required to send a request. If the button does not open an email app, write directly to the address above.

Delete directly in the Android app

  1. Open Fala and sign in to the account whose data you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings and choose Delete all my learning data.
  3. Read the confirmation and select Delete. This cannot be undone.

Your account remains available so you can start practising again.

What is deleted

Deleting all learning data removes your saved conversations, typed or transcribed answers, AI replies, feedback, assessments, learning memory and related AI-content reports from Fala's active database. Practice points, streak history and earned appearances are reset.

Deleting only one conversation removes that conversation and its related learning evidence and reports. Its aggregate reward points remain.

What is kept and for how long

Your Fala account, Google account identifier, email address and active sign-ins are kept so you can continue using Fala. Your nickname, circle memberships and circles you created, reminder and notification settings, and other account preferences are also kept. Account-associated records remain until you change or remove them, or delete your Fala account; active sign-ins expire under the normal session policy.

Fala does not impose an additional retention period on learning data after it is deleted from the active database. Deletion does not immediately erase earlier backups or operational records held by hosting and AI providers. Their retention policies apply; contact support for information about those records. See the privacy policy for provider details.

To also remove your account and its associated records, use Delete your Fala account. Deleting Fala data never deletes your Google account.
