# Fala 0.14.1 — learning at your own pace

Release source: `ef9e41e1db8c940736419dfaab8fe5c2380c298a`. App identity remains `com.fala.app`, with the existing signing identity. This record distinguishes verified publication from pending work.

## Behavior

- Removed automatic difficulty increases after two qualifying conversations and the two-session countdown.
- New conversations retain the most recently practised difficulty. A harder task is an explicit learner choice.
- Optional next-level suggestions require consistent independent practice on different dates and in different contexts, with recent evidence. These are conservative product heuristics, not mastery certification or deadlines.
- Clarified the five levels, removed sentence-count expectations, lowered the internal bias toward long answers and shortened reviewed intermediate examples.
- Reduced lesson vocabulary to two targets at levels 1–2 and up to three later. Familiar patterns, repetition and support remain normal practice.
- Updated both Hebrew and English screens and coaching instructions. Existing progress and saved sessions are preserved. No extra AI request or database migration is needed.
- Translated maintained documentation into English and recorded that preference in `AGENTS.md`. The owner guide is now `docs/owner-guide.md`.

See [the progression specification](../learning-progression.md) for exact rules, educational sources and measurement limits. Topic rotation is unchanged; this release does not add a personalized vocabulary-review scheduler.
