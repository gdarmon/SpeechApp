# Fala closed-testing plan

## Start the test

1. Finish the required Play Console app setup and review the closed release before rollout.
2. Add a group of real Android testers aged 13 and over who can try Fala and give feedback. Teenagers need parent or guardian permission under Fala's current consent flow.
3. Give testers the closed-test opt-in URL from Console. They must use the same Google account as the Play Store. Existing internal testers must leave internal testing before joining the closed test.
4. Confirm that Console shows at least 12 opted-in testers. The 14-day requirement applies to continuous opt-in, not the date you uploaded the bundle or added email addresses. Avoid dropping below the required group.

## What to try

- First session: Google sign-in, English/Hebrew choice, an everyday conversation, question playback and spoken answer suggestions. Try typing as well as holding the microphone.
- Next sessions: capoeira vocabulary, replay/slow playback, correcting recognized words before sending, the ten-reply review and its four or five useful words.
- As practice continues: level selection, recommendations, rewards, available character skins and optional reminders. Try private circles only with people who agree to join.
- At least once: deny microphone permission and use typing; try a poor connection and retry; open the app after closing it. Confirm that the version is visible.
- Privacy and feedback: check that conversations are private, account deletion is accessible, and the AI-report option is discoverable. Use a separate disposable account for destructive deletion checks.

Ask for genuine feedback on what confused testers, where they got stuck and which changes helped. There is no need to manufacture identical daily activity or invent feedback for the application.

## Apply for production

When Console confirms eligibility, answer its application from the actual testing record: recruitment, participation, feedback, changes and readiness. Do not claim a number of active testers or completed fixes that you cannot support. The 14-day minimum enables the application; Google still decides whether production access is granted.

Sources: [testing eligibility](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en), [test track setup](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334?hl=en-GB).
