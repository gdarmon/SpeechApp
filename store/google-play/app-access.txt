Fala uses Google sign-in. There is no separate Fala password, paid subscription, invitation requirement or country restriction in the app code.

1. Open Fala and read the consent information. Online practice is for ages 13 and over; under-18 users need parent or guardian permission.
2. Select Continue with Google and use an eligible Google account on the Android device.
3. Choose English or Hebrew for translations and reply suggestions.
4. Tap Talk. The AI asks a short question in Brazilian Portuguese.
5. Listen to the question or a reply suggestion. Hold to speak, release, review the recognized text and send. Alternatively type a reply; microphone permission is optional when typing.
6. Complete ten replies to see feedback and up to five vocabulary items. Choose Everyday life or ABADA capoeira from the home screen; all five practice levels can be selected.
7. Rewards contains themes and character skins. Bananera is available immediately. Bateba unlocks at 200 XP and Vesoura at 500 XP; these appearances do not restrict lessons.
8. Friends supports private circles and points; it does not provide a person-to-person messaging service.
9. To report an AI reply, open conversation options and select Report this AI reply. To report a summary, use Report this AI review on the review screen.
10. Settings contains learning-data deletion and Fala account deletion. A web deletion route is https://falachatapp.netlify.app/delete-account.html.

No app-owned credentials are needed if the reviewer can use their Google account. Confirm the Google OAuth audience and reviewer access before submission. If Google requires a dedicated review account, the owner must provide reusable access privately in Play Console, not in this repository or chat.
