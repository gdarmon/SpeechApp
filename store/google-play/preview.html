Full description

Know a few Portuguese words, but find it hard to have a conversation? Fala helps you practise speaking Brazilian Portuguese in short, guided conversations.

LISTEN, REPLY, KEEP GOING
Your AI conversation partner starts with a question. Hear it in Brazilian Portuguese and read an English or Hebrew translation. Hold the microphone while you speak, release it, check your words and send. Prefer typing? You can do that too.

A LITTLE HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
See suggestions for what to say, with translations and playback. Replay a question or slow it down. Get brief feedback as you practise, then a focused review after ten replies, with a few pointers and up to five useful words to keep.

START SIMPLE AND GROW
Choose from five practice levels, from first phrases to more connected conversations. Follow Fala's recommendation or select an easier or harder level. Your progress is based on your practice, without a promise of fluency or a formal language qualification.

PORTUGUESE FOR LIFE AND THE RODA
Practise everyday situations and capoeira class vocabulary: instructor directions, movements, instruments, rhythms and the roda. Capoeira knowledge and Portuguese speaking difficulty stay separate, so you can enjoy familiar topics while learning simple phrases.

MAKE PRACTICE YOURS
Earn practice points, follow your streak and unlock themes and capoeira character skins. Join an invite-only circle to compare weekly points with friends. Choose optional reminders at a time that suits you. Character skins change the look of your partner; they share Fala's voice and coaching.

YOUR ACCOUNT, YOUR PROGRESS
Sign in with Google to keep your learning progress together across Android and the Fala website. Manage conversation history, report an AI reply or review, and delete your Fala account in the app or on the web.

Fala is for adults and teenagers aged 13 and over, with parent or guardian permission under 18. An internet connection and Google account are required. Android speech recognition and playback depend on your device and installed Brazilian Portuguese voice. AI and transcription can make mistakes; feedback is not a pronunciation score. Fala is an independent language-learning app and is not an official ABADA-Capoeira product.
