# Capoeira partner artwork

These are the user’s existing CapoeiraMath character skins, reused at their request.

Source repository: `gdarmon/CapoeiraMath`, commit `f444bc7b4ae61367136d51fc5a43d97af304e847` (Add 3D capoeira character skins).
Original assets: `teacher-bananera.png`, `teacher-bateba.png`, `teacher-vesoura.png`.

Unmodified PNG copies are packaged in `public/app/instructors/` and Android’s `drawable-nodpi/instructor_*.png`. Portraits crop only in the UI; full-character previews retain the original artwork. Original character names are preserved. The original child character was not included in this adult/teen partner collection.

The skins represent an appearance for Fala’s AI conversation partner. They do not provide a real instructor’s voice, personal opinions, qualifications or endorsement. Unlock requirements follow practice XP, not a capoeira cord rank.
